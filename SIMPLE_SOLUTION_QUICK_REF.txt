Example: Adding Baghdad

1. Type: "Baghdad" in search box
   
2. Google shows suggestions:
   → Baghdad, Iraq ✅
   
3. Click the suggestion
   
4. Form auto-fills:
   Name: Baghdad ✅
   Arabic: بغداد ✅
   Lat: 33.3152 ✅
   Lng: 44.3661 ✅
   Type: Governorate ✅
   
5. Click: "Save Region"
   
6. Done! 🎉

Takes 10 seconds per region!
