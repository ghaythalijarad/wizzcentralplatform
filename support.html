How do I track my order?

You can track your order in real-time by going to the "My Orders" section in the app. You'll see the current status and estimated delivery time.

How do I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order within 5 minutes of placing it. Go to "My Orders" and tap the "Cancel" button. After preparation starts, cancellation may not be possible.
